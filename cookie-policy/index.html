Cookie Policy

Last updated: 04 December 2025

This Cookie Policy explains how AdfusionSolar (trading as "Synoriya") uses cookies and similar technologies on synoriya.com. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device by a website. We also use similar technologies such as localStorage. These help us operate the site, remember your preferences, and (if you allow it) understand how the site is used.

2. How we classify cookies

We use the following categories in our consent banner:

  • Essential – required for the website to function. These are always active.
  • Analytics – help us understand how visitors use the site. Only loaded if you consent.
Our banner stores your choice in your browser. You can change your decision anytime via the “Cookie Settings” button in the banner.

3. What we actually use on this website

3.1 Essential (always on)

We do not set any non-essential cookies by default. We do store your consent choice in localStorage so that we can remember your preferences and avoid showing the banner on every visit.

Name / Key Type Purpose Provider Retention
synoriya_cookie_consent localStorage Stores your consent choices (essential/analytics) and timestamp. Synoriya Up to 12 months (365 days)

3.2 Analytics (only if you consent)

If you opt in to analytics in the banner, we load Google Analytics 4 (gtag.js). IP anonymization is enabled. No analytics code is loaded until consent is given.

Name Type Purpose Provider Retention
_ga HTTP cookie Distinguishes users for analytics. Google LLC 2 years
_gid HTTP cookie Distinguishes users for analytics (short-term). Google LLC 24 hours
_ga_<container-id> HTTP cookie Persists session state for GA4 property. Google LLC 2 years

For details, visit Google’s privacy info: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and opt-out tool: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

3.3 Embedded scheduling (Calendly)

On our quiz/booking page, we embed a Calendly widget so you can schedule a call. When you load or interact with that widget, Calendly may place functional cookies or use similar technologies that are necessary to provide the scheduling service. These are set by Calendly, not by us.

You can avoid third‑party cookies from Calendly by blocking third‑party cookies in your browser, or by contacting us directly at abdelalissa78@gmail.com to book without using the widget. See Calendly’s privacy policy: calendly.com/privacy.

4. How to control cookies

4.1 Via our banner

Use the Cookie banner on our site to accept only essential cookies or to enable analytics. You can reopen the settings at any time via the “Settings” button in the banner (when shown).

4.2 Browser controls

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • Use private browsing modes to limit storage between sessions.
  • Block third‑party cookies if you do not want embedded content to set cookies.

4.3 Third‑party opt‑outs

  • Google Analytics: browser add‑on
  • Calendly: Privacy Policy

5. International compliance

We aim to comply with EU/EEA and UK cookie rules (ePrivacy/GDPR) and applicable U.S. state privacy laws. We only load analytics after consent and keep a record of your preference.

6. Updates to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Significant changes may also be announced on the website.
